About

25 | Arizona State University. Based in the Phoenix area.

Eli Cottrell

I'm Eli. I'm 25, and currently bouncing around the Phoenix / Peoria / Tempe area inside of Arizona. I attended ASU and became fully enveloped in computer science projects and eventually graduating as a business entrepreneurship major.

I’ve spent the past four years working across project management, program controls, project health and status, and large-scale coordination—first in satellite communications with Iridium, and more recently in semiconductor manufacturing and construction supporting TSMC Arizona.

Despite the scale of the industries I’ve worked in, I’ve always been more drawn to ownership. I built businesses throughout college—some worked, some didn’t—and that process shaped where my interests sit now: entrepreneurship, product design, and product strategy.

I’ve spent more than 3,000 hours in design tools like Figma, Framer, Canva, and Paper, alongside extensive time building with AI. That work has ranged from professional applications and workflows to independent products, experiments, and systems.

Seedcore ↗

I started Seedcore in 2024, right after graduating college. At first, I spent a lot of time trying to figure out what the business actually wanted to become and how I should go about building it. It moved from a B2B ticket analysis tool, to an early-stage consulting agency, and eventually into a productized advisory and intelligence product.

There is still a lot to learn and plenty of hard lessons ahead, but the direction is getting clearer. Improvement is starting to accelerate, the systems are getting tighter, and the pieces of a real business are starting to come together.
