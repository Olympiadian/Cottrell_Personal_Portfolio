Eli Cottrell

I'm Eli. I'm 25, and currently bouncing around the Phoenix, Peoria, and Tempe areas of Arizona. I attended ASU, where I initially immersed myself in computer science before eventually graduating with a degree in Business Entrepreneurship.

For the past four years, I've worked across project management, program controls, project health, and large-scale coordination. I started in satellite communications with Iridium and now work in semiconductor manufacturing and construction supporting TSMC Arizona.

I have a strong predisposition toward problems that don't have a clear or purely technical answer. I'm most interested in work where the path has to be figured out rather than followed: ambiguous products, early-stage strategy, design decisions, growth problems, and situations where judgment + taste matters as much as execution.

I've spent thousands of hours inside tools like Figma, Framer, Canva, and Paper, alongside extensive time building AI applications, automations, and small experimental products. That work ranges from professional workflows and internal tools to independent products, systems, and ideas I've built simply because I wanted to see if they could work.

Seedcore

I started Seedcore in 2024, right after graduating college. At first, I spent a lot of time (probably too much time) trying to figure out what the business actually wanted to become and how I should go about building it. It moved from a B2B ticket analysis tool, to an early-stage consulting agency, and eventually into a productized advisory and intelligence product.

Seedcore is a consulting firm built around a more modern approach to early-stage advisement. Instead of open-ended consulting engagements, we deliver a one-time, productized advisory experience built around research, intelligence, and direct strategic judgment.

The model is designed to be low-friction, highly adaptable, and specific to the business in front of us. The goal is not simply to hand founders information, but to help them understand what matters, where to focus, what to avoid, and how to move forward with significantly more clarity. Seedcore primarily works with first-time business owners, young entrepreneurs, and solo founders who are still navigating the earliest and most uncertain stages of building a company.

Professional

Most of my professional experience has been in project management, program controls, and large-scale coordination. I’ve worked in environments where there are a lot of moving parts, a lot of people involved, and not always a perfectly clear path forward. A big part of my work has been keeping projects organized, understanding what is actually happening, improving visibility, and helping teams make better decisions with the information they have.

At Iridium, I worked on U.S. government programs across forecasting, scheduling, budgeting, project health, and reporting. At KAEKO, I’ve been much closer to active execution, supporting engineering work tied to TSMC Arizona across permitting, client coordination, documentation, field activity, and internal systems. Both roles have taught me a lot about how large projects really function, how quickly things can become unclear, and how valuable good judgment and clear systems are when there are a lot of moving pieces.
